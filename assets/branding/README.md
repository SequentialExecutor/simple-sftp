# Simple SFTP logo

Created with the built-in image generation tool.

Concept: an S-shaped folded document with opposing transfer arrows, in cobalt and mint, on a transparent background.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a polished, clever logo symbol for "Simple SFTP", a friendly, easy-to-use secure file-transfer desktop application.
Concept: a bold geometric letter S formed from two interlocking transfer arrows, with a clean negative-space channel between them. The arrows should feel like files moving smoothly in opposite directions; the whole silhouette should subtly suggest a folded document. Make this one cohesive memorable symbol, with an elegant optical balance and strong silhouette that reads at 24 pixels.
Style: premium minimal flat graphic design, vector-like precision, broad confident shapes, slightly softened corners. Cool, approachable developer-tool identity.
Palette: rich cobalt blue #3456DB as the main color, with a restrained mint-teal #53D6BC accent on one arrow section. No gradients, shadows, 3D, fine outlines, padlocks, shields, globes, servers, ornamental circuitry, or mockups.
Composition: a single large centered standalone symbol, square canvas, generous even transparent padding. No wordmark, letters outside the symbol, captions, labels, watermark, background card, or presentation board. Actual transparent background. This is the final logo asset, not a concept sheet.

## Refinement prompt

Refine this Simple SFTP logo while keeping the same S-shaped folded-document silhouette and opposing transfer arrows. Make it absolutely clean and flat: replace all textures, speckling, grain, mottling, partial transparency and gradients with precisely solid, opaque fills in cobalt #3456DB and mint #53D6BC. Every negative-space arrow and area outside the symbol must be entirely transparent with crisp smooth antialiased boundaries. Preserve the folded upper-right document corner. Simplify to two clear interlocking ribbon shapes with consistent thickness and beautiful optical balance. Center on a square transparent canvas with even padding. No text, background, shadow, mockup, or additional elements. Final professional small-size app logo.
